ART MAMU

I’m Pathan Rahamath Ali — an artist by passion, a designer by profession, and a storyteller at heart. I’m a Visual Designer and Illustrator with over seven years of experience transforming ideas into meaningful visual experiences. Currently, I work as a Visual Designer Lead with Deloitte, exploring the intersection of design, creativity, and communication. From a simple sketch to a complete brand identity, from imaginative characters to visual narratives, I believe every creative idea has a story waiting to be told. My work spans illustration, branding, digital art, graphic design, and visual storytelling, across both digital and print media. Beyond design, I’m passionate about exploring the world of storytelling through comics, storyboarding, animation, and filmmaking. My dream is to become a storyteller who creates worlds, builds memorable characters, and brings meaningful stories to life through visuals. For me, design is more than making things look beautiful. It is about problem solving.
